# SP-Manager 1.0.18

## Locked change
- Fixed save/update success and error notices in Settings so they automatically disappear after the configured message duration.
- Improved Settings notice UI with a compact toast-style design and close button.
- Inventory notices now also auto-dismiss after 5 seconds.

## Scope
Only notification presentation/lifecycle was changed. Existing save, update, calculation, POS, database and other business functions were not intentionally changed.
